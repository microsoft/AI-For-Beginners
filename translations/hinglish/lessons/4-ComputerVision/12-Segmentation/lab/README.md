# Human Body Segmentation

[AI for Beginners Curriculum](https://github.com/microsoft/ai-for-beginners) se Lab Assignment.

## Task

Video production mein, jaise weather forecasting mein, humein aksar camera se human figure ko cut out karke kisi doosre footage par lagana hota hai. Yeh aam taur par **chroma key** techniques ka use karke kiya jaata hai, jisme ek person ko uniform color background ke saamne film kiya jaata hai, jise baad mein hata diya jaata hai. Is lab mein, hum ek neural network model ko train karenge taaki human figure ko cut out kiya ja sake.

## Dataset

Hum Kaggle par available [Segmentation Full Body MADS Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tapakah68/segmentation-full-body-mads-dataset) ka use karenge. Is dataset ko Kaggle se manually download kijiye.

## Starting Notebook

Lab shuru karne ke liye [BodySegmentation.ipynb](../../../../../../lessons/4-ComputerVision/12-Segmentation/lab/BodySegmentation.ipynb) kholiye.

## Takeaway

Body segmentation un common tasks mein se ek hai jo hum logon ki images ke saath kar sakte hain. Doosre important tasks mein **skeleton detection** aur **pose detection** shaamil hain. Yeh dekhne ke liye ki in tasks ko kaise implement kiya ja sakta hai, [OpenPose](https://github.com/CMU-Perceptual-Computing-Lab/openpose) library par nazar daaliye.
