# NetLogo Assignment

NetLogo ki library mein se koi ek model lijiye aur use kisi real-life situation ko jitna ho sake accurately simulate karne ke liye use kijiye. Ek achha example yeh hoga ki optional visualization folder mein maujood virus model ko is tarah modify kiya jaye ki woh dikha sake ki ise COVID-19 ke spread ko model karne ke liye kaise use kiya ja sakta hai. Kya aap ek aisa model bana sakte hain jo real life mein virus ke spread ki nakal karta ho?

Apna kaam dikhane ke liye ek copy save kijiye aur ek video demo banaiye, jisme explain kiya gaya ho ki yeh model real-world situation se kaise juda hua hai.
