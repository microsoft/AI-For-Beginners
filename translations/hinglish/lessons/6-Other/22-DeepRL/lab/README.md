## Environment

Mountain Car environment mein ek car hoti hai jo ek valley mein phansi hoti hai. Aapka goal hai valley se bahar kudkar flag tak pahunchna. Jo actions aap kar sakte hain, woh hain left taraf accelerate karna, right taraf accelerate karna, ya kuch bhi na karna. Aap car ki position ko x-axis par aur uski velocity ko observe kar sakte hain.

## Starter Notebook

Is lab ko shuru karne ke liye [MountainCar.ipynb](../../../../../../lessons/6-Other/22-DeepRL/lab/MountainCar.ipynb) kholiye.

## Main Takeaway

Is lab ke dauran aapko yeh samajh aana chahiye ki RL algorithms ko kisi naye environment mein adapt karna aksar kaafi simple hota hai, kyunki OpenAI Gym ka sabhi environments ke liye ek uniform interface hota hai, aur algorithms aam taur par environment ki nature par zyada depend nahi karte. Aap Python code ko is tarah restructure bhi kar sakte hain ki koi bhi environment RL algorithm ko parameter ke roop mein pass kiya ja sake.
